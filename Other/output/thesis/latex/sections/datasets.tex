\section{Datasets}
\label{sec:datasets}

Table~\ref{tab:datasets} summarizes the four public corpora used in the thesis narrative. The repository additionally profiles processed and transformed Parquet under \path{data/} with rollups in \path{output/dataset analysis/}.

Across these sources, missingness is generally low and duplicate rates are small in the processed slices; what drives modeling choices is the spread of document length (truncation limits for transformers), class priors (Sentiment140 is strongly skewed, on the order of one-quarter vs.\ three-quarters in the thesis profile), and stance ambiguity in multi-class Twitter tasks. Those factors motivate class weighting, balanced sampling or focal-style objectives where used, and careful calibration of maximum sequence length per expert family.

Merged corpus statistics and per-file profiles appear in \path{output/dataset analysis/alignment_report.md} and per-file \path{report.md} files (IMDB, Amazon sample, Sentiment140, Yelp, HRAST stem slice, etc.). The combined processed/transformed Parquet merge in the repository profile reaches on the order of \textbf{9.5M} rows with matching \path{source_stem} distributions between processed and transformed exports (see the implementation report). The HRAST stem slice used for lightweight per-config evaluation is much smaller (order $10^3$--$10^4$ rows depending on export) and is documented under \path{data/processed/by_source_stem/} and \path{output/dataset analysis/}.
