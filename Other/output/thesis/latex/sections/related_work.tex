\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Prior art spans CNN--LSTM hybrids~\cite{ref12}, class-balanced social sentiment~\cite{ref14}, deep ensembles for social text~\cite{ref15}, multilingual ensembles~\cite{ref13}, and stacking with diverse bases~\cite{ref16,ref17}. Chain-of-thought and multi-step reasoning~\cite{ref09,ref18,ref19,ref20,ref21} motivate explicit structure; HRMs~\cite{ref08} organize multi-level reasoning. Ensemble theory and MoE / sparse routing~\cite{ref22,ref23,ref24,ref25,ref26,ref27,ref28,ref29} underpin gating. Domain adaptation and robustness~\cite{ref05,ref06,ref30} motivate heterogeneous experts. Classic sentiment baselines (e.g., document-level ML~\cite{ref33}, CNN sentence encoders~\cite{ref35}, tree-structured composition~\cite{ref34}) frame what ``strong'' single-model performance looks like before large encoders. Review mining~\cite{ref31} and phrase-level polarity~\cite{ref32} illustrate domain and granularity effects that persist in social text. This implementation targets integration of HRM $+$ ML $+$ DL under one evaluation protocol rather than a new backbone architecture.
