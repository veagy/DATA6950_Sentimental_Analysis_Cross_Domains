The Multi-Domain Challenge:
Nuance Deficiency: Standard Transformers often fail at pragmatic reasoning (sarcasm, irony).
Domain Fragility: Models trained on broad data (IMDB) degrade in specialized fields (Healthcare, Finance).
The Hybrid Hypothesis: Combining Classical ML Rigor, Deep Learning Context, and Hierarchical Reasoning creates a robust "Team
of Experts".
Strategic Objectives:
Architecture: A modular MoE system with dynamic gating and HRM reasoning.
Performance: Target and achieve ≥3.5 F1 improvement via Stacking/MoE.
Rigorous Implementation: Building from-scratch, optimized libraries for reproducibility.

--- SLIDE 7 ---
Core Research Findings:
Top Performer: The STACK1 Ensemble is the "Champion" model,
achieving a peak accuracy of 0.892 on IMDB, representing a 3.5%
improvement over the baseline.
Architectural Efficiency: While Transformers and RNNs were
tested, Optimized SVMs and Ensemble Stacking consistently
yielded the highest scores across IMDB and Amazon datasets.
The Complexity Gap: There is a visible performance "tax" when
moving from Binary to Ternary classification; Macro-F1 scores
typically drop as class complexity increases, with the notable
exception of the Yelp dataset.
Reliability Analysis: The correlation between Accuracy and
Macro-F1 remains tight for most models, though the Yelp Binary
model shows a significant "Robustness Gap," indicating potential
sensitivity to class imbalance.

The Snapshot of Final Completion:

Semantic → Pragmatic) using the optimized Attention library.

Training and Routing
Step1: Implementing training of multiple machine learning, deep learning, hrm models
individually on binary, terinary labels on each dataset separately.
Step2: Validating these models on the datasets separately and getting F1-scores for
them.
Step3: Connecting the models based on the proposed mixture of experts architecture,
baselines (defined in phase-I of project), literature-review-models (defined in phase-I
of this project) and vadiating them on datasets and comparing F1-scores from the
step-2.
Step-4: doing fine-tuning on this MoE (mixture of experts architecture) to finish the
final ensemble training for all proposed models and comparing the final scores.
for robustness and cross-domain analysis all datasets are randomly sampled training
procedure from step-1 to step-4 is applied.
Final ablation studies are done on the proposed MoE models.
